## STEP 4 — COMPETITIVE POOL TEARDOWN

- **Shokz:** global "Be Open" positioning; CES 2026 launch of OpenFit Pro (open-ear noise reduction without sealing the canal); customer testimonials built entirely around situational-awareness-while-running language ("I can listen to my music and still be aware of my surroundings at all times during my runs"). **Steal:** the plain, first-person testimonial caption voice — it reads as organic, not a spec pitch, and fits our Observer/Declaration modes cleanly. **Avoid:** their vocabulary leans hard on "aware/safe" — we keep our own approved vocabulary (§06 bans "safe" as clinical/overused) and stay curious rather than safety-first in tone.
- **Noise:** just launched the "ALT" ecosystem, including **ALT Buds Open** — open-ear earbuds carrying a Red Dot Design Award, marketed on "comfort and awareness of surroundings." This is a homegrown competitor now selling the same category promise we do. Historically Noise also uses stunt-proof demos (bungee-jumping OWS earbuds) and star athlete/cricket ambassadors. **Steal:** the extreme-scenario "proof it stays on and stays open" demo mechanic — usable for an on-brand real-world stress-test Scene without a stunt budget. **Avoid:** cricket/celebrity-ambassador spend is out of our organic, no-spend lane and stop-list-adjacent; do not name Noise in creative.
- **boAt:** celebrity- and cricket-led campaigns (Kartik Aaryan, Kiara Advani, six IPL teams), the #DoWhatFloatsYourBOAT UGC hashtag challenge, and even a virtual influencer (KYRA) for product launches. **Steal:** the UGC hashtag-challenge mechanic — many small authentic voices telling the same story compounds reach without one big production. **Avoid:** celebrity-name-dropping and big-budget ambassador spend are out of scope for this organic, no-spend lane.
- **Boult:** wins through volume, not one big campaign — hundreds of micro-influencers, the #GetSetBoult story-shoutout wave (122 creators), aggressive price-value positioning, with celebrity ambassadors (Saif Ali Khan, Suryakumar Yadav) reserved for the top tier only. **Steal:** consistency and distributed volume beating a single hero campaign — supports leaning into a raw, repeatable single-take Reel format rather than one polished hero asset. **Avoid:** UGC-repost-without-a-brand-voice-layer is explicitly on our stop list; price-war messaging doesn't fit our confident-not-shouty brand voice (§05).

**1–2 stealable angles this cycle:** (1) Shokz's plain first-person testimonial caption voice, redirected into Observer/Declaration copy for IC-028/IC-029; (2) the general lesson from boAt/Boult that many small, raw, authentic pieces of content beat one polished hero asset — directly informs IC-030's single-take, no-licensed-audio Reel. **What to avoid across all four:** celebrity/cricket-ambassador dependency, hashtag-stacking, UGC-repost without a brand-voice caption, and — new this cycle — naming Noise's ALT Buds Open or otherwise engaging their open-ear claim directly in creative (stop-list: no competitor naming).

### IC-029 · CAROUSEL — Lifestyle-presence × open-ear (Declaration) — ◆ RECOMMENDED LEAD

- **Bucket + tribe:** lifestyle-presence × open-ear · Tribe D.
- **Metric target:** saves ≥20 — hits the institutional 20+-save secondary-distribution trigger (decisive = saves).
- **Visual:** Canva for slides 1–6 & 8 (text on Chalk/Frost, primary typeface, no Bold weight); S7 = real SafeBuds on-ear photo (`Product Images/` or Drive library) — never AI-generated device.

### IC-030 · REEL — Lifestyle-presence (Scene)
- **Hook (0–3s, works sound-off):** one continuous handheld take, no cuts — subject steps out of a home into a real evening street/rooftop scene, SafeBuds/Comm 2.0 already on-ear.

Documentary-style throughout, per §08 Rule 3 (real moments, not posed) and this week's platform-wide shift toward raw over polished content.
- **Payoff (15s+, on-screen text only, no CTA in video):** "You didn't miss a single second of either."
