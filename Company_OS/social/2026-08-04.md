## STEP 4 — COMPETITIVE POOL TEARDOWN

- **Shokz:** reveal-tease Reels ("Something new is taking shape… Can you guess?"), athlete-ambassador Reels, CES-recap hashtag stacks (`#ces #ces2026 #boneinduction`). **Steal:** the curiosity-gap product-tease hook. **Avoid:** heavy hashtag-stacking — Metricool's 2026 data shows hashtag-heavy posts now get 31% fewer views/interactions, so this is a demoted tactic, not a model to copy.
- **boAt:** celebrity/influencer-led campaigns timed to civic/cultural moments (Republic Day "AatmaNirbhar Bharat," Women's Day #WatchHerPlay, IPL tie-ins), "boAthead" community identity. **Steal:** tying a values-based message to a live cultural moment rather than a bare product post. **Avoid:** celebrity name-dropping (outside our budget and stop-list-adjacent) and generic patriotic-lite messaging without an original angle — exactly what our stop list already guards against.
- **Noise (@go_noise):** dry, topical pun-driven wit tied to live cultural moments (a cricket World Cup pun, an April Fools' single-earbud gag) — "Greatness isn't born in silence. It's made of Noise." **Steal:** the *timing discipline* of dry Wit-mode copy landing on a live cultural beat. **Avoid:** the mechanic depends on their brand name literally being a pun on "noise" — not transferable word-for-word, only the technique is.
- **Boult:** high-volume micro-influencer UGC, ratings/reviews as content proof, deep non-metro penetration; celebrity ambassadors (Suryakumar Yadav, Saif Ali Khan) reserved for the top tier only. **Steal:** consistency and volume beating one single big campaign. **Avoid:** UGC-repost-without-a-brand-voice-layer is explicitly on our stop list, and celebrity-tier spend is out of scope for this lane.

**1–2 stealable angles this cycle:** (1) the curiosity-gap "tease" hook style (Shokz), redirected toward a non-product reveal; (2) landing a witty, self-aware line on a live cultural beat (Noise's technique) — used in IC-027's affectionate generational joke. **What to avoid across all four:** celebrity/name-drop dependency, hashtag-stacking, UGC-repost without a brand-voice caption, and generic festival/civic posts without a sharp original angle.
