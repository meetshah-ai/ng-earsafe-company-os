### Option B — P2 Meta Ads / D2C playbook

**Hook (127 chars):**
> We scaled a Meta ad's budget 35% and its cost-per-purchase went DOWN 39%. Here's the account structure that made that possible.

**Thesis:** The first-ever Meta Ads numbers post on this page. Teaches the actual account-structure rule behind a real, dated scale decision, in the "we cut CPP X% by doing Y" register the constitution calls for — never the generic "5 tips" post.

**Bucket:** P2. **Idea link:** NEW — first LinkedIn post built directly from a meta-ads lane finding (Techopedia SafeBuds scale, confirmed 2026-07-03). No REJECTED item is repeated.

**Body (1,066 chars):**
> Most people assume: scale the budget, watch efficiency drop.
>
> Two weeks ago we ran the opposite experiment on purpose.
>
> We took our best-performing SafeBuds creative — a reviewer-style Reel, not a feature ad — and scaled its daily budget by 35%.
>
> The textbook worry: cost-per-purchase creeps up as the algorithm hunts for a thinner slice of the same audience.
>
> What actually happened:
>
> → Spend up 35%
> → Cost-per-purchase down 39%
> → ROAS climbed from 12.4x to 14.3x
> → Ad frequency eased, it didn't spike
>
> Why scaling didn't break this one, in one line: we didn't scale a tired creative. We scaled the one still earning fresh reach every week.
>
> The account rule we're taking into the next scale decision:
>
> A creative earns a budget increase only after it clears two bars in the same week — rising reach AND falling frequency. Miss either one, and the "obvious" scale becomes next week's kill.
>
> Most Meta ads advice out there is generic. Ours is just what we're watching happen in our own ads manager, this week.
>
> We'll post the next read when we push the budget again.

**First comment (soft CTA, no link needed):** "If you want the account-structure rule as a one-slide checklist, say the word and I'll post it separately."

**Hashtags:** #MetaAds #PerformanceMarketing #D2C #GrowthMarketing #FounderBuildingInPublic
