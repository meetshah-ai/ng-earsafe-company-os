`sessionCampaignName` scatters Meta traffic under non-obvious tags — "Broad Interest Based-24 Jul 26"

string), not the plain campaign name; "Openwire_5 Aug 26" lands under **"Openwire LAL 1%_5 Aug 26"**.

not prefix matching — an earlier draft of the compute script used `.startswith()` and silently mis-priced
Broad Interest's directional ROAS at 0.26× before this was caught and fixed mid-run. Also found: a
`{{campaign.name}}`/`{{ad.name}}` unresolved-template-variable tagging bug losing ₹7,058 (cur7d) +
₹5,798+₹1,829 (prior7d) of real Meta revenue to broken dynamic UTM params — distinct from the known
ES Comm 2 (MA-037) and ES Lite (ad-name-as-campaign) tagging bugs, not yet drafted as its own FIX; noting
here for the next cycle to formalise if it recurs.
