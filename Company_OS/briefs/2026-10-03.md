## The read
MER fell 22% day-over-day as net sales dropped 37% (43 orders vs 74 the day before), even though
Meta's own ROAS rose 28.2% week-over-week on strong Broad Interest campaign performance. The PDP
table shows a real gap: SafeBuds carries 65% of all PDP sessions but converts at just 1.27%, while
the smaller Air Conduction page (36 sessions) converts over 2x higher at 2.78% — worth a look at
SafeBuds' page experience given the traffic weight it carries. Seven sessions landed on a 404 page
yesterday — a small number, but paid clicks being wasted either way. Platforms' own claimed ROAS
overstates true MER by only 2.7%, well under the usual 10–25% band — a swing this far below normal
is itself worth watching, as it can mean a platform's conversion tracking has gone quiet rather than
attribution being healthy. Returns ticked up to a 0.64% 7-day rate (from 0.00% the week before),
traced to a single ₹5,647 refund on 27 Sep.
→ One thing to act on: Look at the SafeBuds PDP page experience — it carries most of the paid
traffic but converts well below smaller product pages.
