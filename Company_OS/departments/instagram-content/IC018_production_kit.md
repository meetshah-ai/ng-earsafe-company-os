## FORMAT & GLOBAL STYLE
- **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical 1080×1920 · 2 clips, ~6–7s each, ~13s total.
- **Look:** realistic phone-shot home video, NOT glossy/commercial. Dim room, one lamp or phone
  glow only — it is a 1–2am kickoff, the room should read as genuinely late.
- **Energy:** natural real-time motion, comedic timing, handheld with subtle shake, micro-movements
  (blinks, breathing, flinches). Single continuous take per clip, no internal cuts.
- **Negative prompt:** `hard cuts, scene transition, jump cut, montage, split screen, slow motion,
  warped hands, distorted face, morphing, extra fingers, on-screen text, watermark, brand logos,
  team crests, player likeness, glossy commercial look`
- **Brand safety (stop-list):** no team jerseys/crests, no broadcast footage, no player names or
  likeness anywhere in frame or copy. The comedy is 100% about the *viewer's* behaviour, never the
  match itself — this keeps it clear of FIFA/team IP and the "no public figure" rule.
