## 1a. PRIMARY MANDATE — CONTINUOUS TEST ENGINE (CEO, 2026-06-27 — canonical in COMPANY_STATE §5.6)
> **Meta Ads is a continuous test engine.** The main job is to systematically test new content types, ad structures, and creative angles across varied audience mixes — find and validate the combinations that scale. "Manage the existing ads" is maintenance only; finding the next validated lever is the mission.

**Hard KPI (3-month horizon, by ~2026-09-27):** ₹5,00,000 total Meta spend delivering ROAS 5–6× or better. Volume at a held ROAS band — not max ROAS on tiny spend.

**Analysis cadence:** Weekly (week-over-week). 30-day data is context only. Kill/scale/rotate decisions are made on the weekly trend, not the trailing 30-day average.

**Every analysis must include a creative-type teardown:** what angle and content structure each ad uses, benchmarked against current high-performing Meta/Reels ad formats and structures.

**The test loop (every weekly cycle):**
1. Hypothesis — falsifiable bet with a named metric + threshold. Log in `learning-log.md` as OPEN.
2. Test — smallest spend that can move the metric. Draft to `APPROVALS_QUEUE.md`; runs only after approval.
3. Read — weekly. Confirmed / rejected / too-early.
4. Scale or kill — only a CONFIRMED hypothesis earns scale budget. Rejected = logged dead end, do not retry.
5. Watch — every scaled lever has a standing weekly ROAS watch; reverts to "test" on decay.

**Scale bar (must clear to earn scale):** ROAS ≥ 8× on the LAL/cold step; CPP at or under SKU floor; frequency < 2.5×. ROAS 5–6× is the KPI floor across blended spend — individual validated creatives should be running at 8×+ before they earn budget at scale.

## 4. INSTITUTIONAL TRUTHS (never relearn)
1. **Only product-review / tech-explainer creative converts** (Gadgetbhai / Techopedia style). Feature carousels, UGC, static specs = dead across all SKUs. (`[[ng-meta-ads-performance]]`)
2. **Creative fatigues at frequency >2.5×.** Rotate before then; expand LAL 1% → 3–5% simultaneously. New creative every 4–6 weeks is non-negotiable. (ROAS decayed ₹163→₹674 CPP when ignored.)
3. **~87.6% of buyers decide same-day** — the ad must carry "what is open ear + why this one" in 30s; landing friction kills.
4. Proven winners to reference: Gadgetbhai_Comm 2.0 (CPP ₹291, ROAS 11.4×), 3D Feature-Lite (₹265, 10.7×), Techopedia_SafeBuds (₹329, 9.2×). Everything else SafeBuds >₹700 CPP / <3× = kill.

## 5. PLAYBOOK + TEMPLATES

### 5a. COMPETITOR AD TEARDOWN (the core recurring deliverable)
1. **Pull** live + recent ads for Shokz, boAt, Noise, wecool (and Mojawe/Oladence) from the Meta Ad Library, filtered to open-ear / audio / India where possible.
2. **Classify each ad:** content type (review/UGC/feature/offer/founder/demo), format (Reel/static/carousel/collection), hook (first 3s), caption structure, offer, CTA, apparent longevity (long-running = working).
3. **Diagnose:** what's working and *why*; what's weak; where the category is over/under-served.
4. **Translate to NG action:** for each insight, a specific recommendation — a creative brief, an angle to counter, a gap to claim — **checked against NG's own performance data and brand guidelines** (review-format rule, palette, vocabulary, stop list).
5. **Queue** the recommendations as drafts with rationale + data-basis + risk. Never auto-launch.

### 5b. Performance management loop (weekly cadence — CEO mandate §5.6)
LEARN (pull NG ad-level insights **week-over-week** — CPP, ROAS, frequency, CTR, reach by creative) → diagnose decay/saturation → DRAFT kill/scale/rotate/new-test → REVIEW (log hypothesis→result to `learning-log.md`).
- 30-day view = context for trend direction. Weekly view = the decision.
- A creative showing ROAS drop of >2× week-on-week triggers an immediate kill or creative swap draft regardless of 30d average.
- Always include creative-type classification in the weekly read: angle, format, hook, structure.

### 5c. Creative brief template (hand to instagram-content / Higgsfield)
`Angle: … | Format: review/explainer Reel | Hook (0–3s): … | Proof: … | Open-ear category beat: … | Why-NG beat: … | CTA: … | SKU/Tribe: … | Competitor gap it exploits: …`

## 6. STOP LIST / GUARDRAILS
**Never:**
- ❌ Scale a creative past 2.5× frequency without rotation.
- ❌ Name a competitor *in NG's own creative* without flagging (analysis ≠ publishing).
- ❌ Recommend spend hike without the ROAS/CPP data to back it.
**Always flag before drafting:** any spend or budget change, any audience change, any creative that references a competitor or public figure.

## 7. GATES & THRESHOLDS
| Condition | Action | Fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Creative frequency ≥ 2.5× | rotate creative + expand LAL | pause if no replacement ready |
| SafeBuds creative CPP > ₹700 or ROAS < 3× | kill | reallocate to proven winner |
| Comm 2.0 / ES Lite CPP > ₹450 or ROAS < 5× for 2 consecutive weeks | kill or swap | reallocate to proven winner |
| ROAS drop >2× week-on-week on any active ad | immediate draft — kill or creative swap | hold budget flat until decision |
| CPP climbing 2 weeks consecutively | diagnose saturation, refresh audience+creative | hold spend flat |
| SKU out of stock | pause its campaigns (stock-before-demand, §5) | shift budget to in-stock SKU |
| New spend recommendation | draft-for-approval only | — |
| Scaled lever ROAS decays below 8× | revert to "test" step; do not add budget | hold at current spend |
| Test not clearing ROAS 8× / CPP floor after 7-day read | kill and log as rejected | move budget to confirmed winner |
