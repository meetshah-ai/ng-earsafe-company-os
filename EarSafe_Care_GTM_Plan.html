02Where we play — target market

Primary buyer: the adult child (not the patient)

In India the hearing aid is researched, chosen and paid for by the adult child for a parent. They are online, low on stigma, time-poor, guilt-motivated, and already partly inside EarSafe's audience. We market to the child; the parent is the user.

Geography (pilot)

One metro cluster where our existing audience density, English/Hindi reach, and last-mile logistics are strongest — Bangalore + Hyderabad as the lead candidate (tech-corridor adult children, high online buying, our strongest organic footprint). Expand on proven economics, not ambition.

The beachhead, precisely

Moderate-loss parents, bought online by their 30–45 children in metro India, served by TINY (invisible + rechargeable) and Radius 16 (16-channel value). Win this narrow wedge before widening.

Pricing posture: value-leader, not discounter

  • Hold MRP. Win on spec-per-rupee + the benefit stack, not headline discounts — this protects the margin the CAC gate depends on.
  • Benefit stack as the lever (Earkart-funded): free insurance, 0% no-cost EMI, 4 free senior-audiologist consults, exchange/upgrade, free LED dehumidifier on hero SKUs.
  • Honesty rule: lead claims with rechargeable / invisible / pair / certified / warranty; use channel-count as support only (budget "16ch" ≠ premium 16ch).
  • Stay ≤₹30K. We do not fight where Bluetooth/streaming decides the sale (Earkart has none) — that's Apple/Signia territory.

09Operating model — who owns what

EarSafe owns

Brand & sub-brand · demand generation · acquisition cost · the online experience (site, screening UX, WhatsApp, content) · first-line lead management.

Earkart owns

Audiology & remote diagnosis (Omni) · device fitting · warranty & returns liability · clinical fulfilment & service · CDSCO licensing · the benefit stack.

The protecting principle

EarSafe earns device margin for bringing qualified demand; margin never moves without clinical responsibility moving with it. Confirm in the partnership agreement before any spend: margin split, fitting/warranty/returns liability on Earkart, CDSCO indemnity, MRP floor, lead-ownership rules, audiologist consult SLA.

10The 90-day pilot

Weeks 1–2 · Setup

Terms + foundations

Sign partnership terms (liability, SLA, floor, indemnity). Stand up EarSafe Care pages, separate pixel/campaigns, Omni screening integration, WhatsApp flow.

Weeks 2–4 · Content + SEO

Trust spine live

Publish money-keyword hub + buyer guides; build paid creative; seed owned audience to screening.

Weeks 4–8 · Pilot live

Spend, capped

One metro cluster, hero TINY + Radius 16, capped media. Daily funnel tracking against the gate.

Week 6 · Day-45 read

First gate

CAC, GM, return rate vs targets. Continue / fix / kill.

Week 12 · Day-90 read

Scale decision

If gate clears: widen geography + SKUs. Only then does full clinical retail (Move 3) open.

Parallel quick-win (no clinical risk)

Launch Bluup earplugs + accessories under the core EarSafe brand immediately — on-brand "safe listening" prevention product, 50% margin, builds the prevention→correction bridge while the pilot stands up.
