01 — The Problem

In-ear was never built for all day.

If you wear earbuds from your first call to your last, the thing sealing your ear is quietly working against you.

01

It shuts you off

Sealed buds block the room — you miss the client beside you, the colleague, the announcement.

02

It wears you out

Hours of canal pressure mean fatigue and a dull ring by evening — ears that need a break you can't take.

03

It isn't built for the heat

Sweat, slippage and hygiene issues from sealing your ear canal shut for hours at a time.
