### 1.3 OPEN EAR EDUCATION (for confused / skeptical customers)

**"It doesn't block sound — is it broken?"**

```
Nope, that's exactly how it's supposed to work 😊

Open ear means the speaker sits outside your ear canal — not inside it. So you hear your audio AND your surroundings at the same time.

Takes about 10 minutes to get used to. After that, most people can't go back to regular earphones.

Is the sound itself coming through clearly?
```

---

**"How is this different from normal earphones?"**

```
Normal earphones seal your ear canal — blocks outside sound, traps heat and bacteria, creates pressure after a few hours.

EarSafe sits outside the ear. Canal stays open. You hear your audio + the world around you.

Different use case, different experience. What made you look at open ear?
```

---

**"Will it fall off? How does it stay on?"**

```
Hook design — it loops over the ear and stays. Most people forget they're wearing it.

SafeBuds weighs 9 grams per side. Comm 2.0 has a different fit (headset style). ES Pro uses a wraparound titanium band.

Which product are you asking about?
```

### 1.4 SAFEGUARDS — THE BIG PRODUCT QUESTIONS

**SphereLingo™ / Translation features (SafeBuds)**

```
SphereLingo™ is SafeBuds' built-in AI. Real-time translation in 100+ languages — it translates what someone is saying into your language as they speak.

Also does live note-taking during meetings. You speak, it transcribes.

Works through the SafeBuds companion app (iOS + Android). Setup takes about 2 minutes.
```

---

**"Is there noise cancellation?"**

```
No active noise cancellation — and that's by design.

Open ear is the opposite philosophy. Instead of blocking the world, it keeps you in it. Great sound quality, full awareness of your surroundings.

If you need to shut everything out — that's a different type of product and we're honest about that.
```

---

**"Can I use it for workouts / is it waterproof?"**

```
Depends on which one:

ES Pro — IP68. Swimming, rain, sweat. Built for it.
SafeBuds — IPX4. Sweat and light splash proof. Fine for gym, not swimming.
Comm 2.0 — check with us on the variant you're looking at.
```

### 1.7 TECHNICAL TROUBLESHOOTING

**Not pairing / Bluetooth issue**

```
Let's fix this. Try these steps:

1. Forget the device on your phone's Bluetooth settings
2. Hold the power button on the earphones for 10 seconds to reset
3. Put in pairing mode again (usually hold power until light flashes)
4. Search fresh on your phone

Does that work?
```

*If still not resolved:*

```
Okay let's escalate this. Share your order number and a quick video of what's happening — I'll get our tech team on it today.
```

---

**Mic not working on calls**

```
A few things to check:

- Is the device selected as the audio output on your phone / laptop during the call?
- On Comm 2.0, make sure the mic boom is extended forward (not folded back)
- Try a test call through your phone's default dialer first

Which app / device are you on?
```

---

**SafeBuds app / SphereLingo™ not working**

```
A few things:

- App downloaded? It's called "SafeBuds" on the App Store / Play Store
- Buds connected to app (not just Bluetooth) — the app has its own pairing step
- Language selected in app before starting translation?

What step is it getting stuck at?
```

---

**Battery draining fast**

```
A couple of checks:

- Buds fully charged? First charge should be 1 full hour before use
- If battery indicator shows 100% but drains in under 2 hours — that's worth logging as a warranty issue

Share your order number and I'll note it down.
```
